### Hi, I'm Giovanni 👋

Backend Software Engineer and **Tech Lead at Aetheos** in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. I build Go and PostgreSQL backends on Google Cloud (async pipelines, partner APIs, payments), and I make games with Godot at [Bush Dog Games](https://bushdoggames.github.io).

- 🏠 **Aetheos Home**: real-estate photography app on the App Store and Google Play. I designed its architecture and built most of the Go backend, including an AI image-editing pipeline that cut infrastructure and AI costs by 90%.
- 🎮 **[Extreme Super Jumper](https://bush-dog-games.itch.io/extreme-super-jumper)**: 2D arcade puzzle-platformer for PC and browser, built in Godot. I was the lead developer.
- 🧹 **[temp-deleter-go](https://github.com/giomascitelli/temp-deleter-go)**: fast cross-platform temp-file cleaner in Go, with concurrent processing.
